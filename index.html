How verification works

Every printed certificate carries a unique QR code in the lower-left area of the document. Scanning it with any phone camera opens the matching verification page on this portal — a quick way to confirm the holder's identity, registration number, issue and expiry dates, and certificate serial.

  • Check that the URL in the browser starts with verify.heightsafety.co.za/cert/
  • Compare the printed name, ID and registration number with what's shown on the page
  • Confirm the status banner reads Valid and the expiry date has not passed
